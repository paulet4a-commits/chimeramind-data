Workflow

Hiring research

Collect jobs from company career sites, research LinkedIn postings and monitor hiring signals alongside remote vacancies.

  1. Career Site Jobs API (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday +1)

    Career Site Jobs API finds which ATS a company uses (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday) from its domain or name and returns its open jobs in one unified format, $3 per 1,000 jobs.

  2. LinkedIn Jobs Scraper

    LinkedIn Jobs Scraper returns public job listings from LinkedIn by keywords, location, and filters. Scrape job titles, companies, salaries, descriptions, and applicant counts from LinkedIn's public guest job search API.

  3. Hiring Signals Scraper (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable)

    Scrape company job postings and hiring signals from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workable job boards — one row per job, or one hiring summary per company.

  4. Remote Jobs Aggregator (RemoteOK, WWR, Hacker News)

    Merge and de-duplicate remote job listings from RemoteOK, We Work Remotely and Hacker News' Who is Hiring thread into one clean row per job — title, company, salary, tags and apply link.

These are suggested research steps. Review each input schema and map the output fields between steps; each tool is billed separately at its listed price.
